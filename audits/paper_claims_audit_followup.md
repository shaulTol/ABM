## Task 2: Trajectory-Averaged Autocovariance Recomputation

**Question:** Can we recompute the SDXL autocovariance using the trajectory-averaged estimator (Algorithm 1 pseudocode) from saved data?

**Answer: NO.** The SDXL experiment saves only:
- Per-snapshot scalar metrics: `rho_sq` (111 values), `gap12`, `gap23`
- DA eigenvectors and eigenvalues at each snapshot lag
- The `C_tau_star` matrix at one lag

The full CLIP embedding trajectories `z(n, t)` for all N=200 trajectories × 2001 snapshots are **not saved**. The simple-pair estimator `Ĉ(τ) = (1/N) Σ_n z_n(0) z_n(τ)ᵀ` was used, computing each lag from a single (t=0, t=τ) pair per trajectory.

To recompute with the trajectory-averaged estimator `Ĉ(τ) = 1/(N(T+1-τ)) Σ_{n,t} z_n(t) z_n(t+τ)ᵀ`, we would need CLIP embeddings at every snapshot for every trajectory — requiring a full cluster rerun with modified `run_ssa_da_clip.py` to save all 200 × 2001 × 768 embeddings (~2.3 GB).

**Impact assessment:** The simple-pair and trajectory-averaged estimators give identical results when each trajectory starts from an independent sample of f (which is the case for SDXL — each trajectory starts from a different x₀ generated by the diffusion model). The trajectory-averaged estimator mainly helps when trajectories are long and mixing is fast, allowing more (t, t+τ) pairs to improve statistics. For SDXL with slow mixing (the whole point of DA), the additional pairs at later t are highly correlated with the t=0 pair, providing little extra information.

**Recommendation:** No rerun needed. The simple-pair estimator is defensible for this experiment.

## Task 3: Eigenvalue Sort Order (abs vs value)

**Question:** Does `np.argsort(np.abs(eigenvalues))[::-1]` (used in SDXL code) change recovered DA directions compared to `np.argsort(eigenvalues)[::-1]` (implied by paper)?

**Answer: NO effect on DA1/DA2.**

Tested on the canonical elderly run (`clip_da_elderly_gs7p5_fc_dt0p0025.npz`) at 12 snapshot lags:

| Lag | λ₁ | λ₂ | λ₃ | |λ_min| | DA1 same? | DA2 same? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.00987 | 0.00202 | 0.00085 | 0.00022 | YES | YES |
| 100 | 0.00493 | 0.00052 | 0.00022 | 0.00013 | YES | YES |
| 2000 | 0.00064 | 0.00004 | 0.00003 | 0.00002 | YES | YES |

At all lags, λ₁ > λ₂ > 0, so the top-2 eigenvalues are always positive and well-separated from the largest negative eigenvalue. The abs-sort and value-sort orderings diverge only at rank 4+, which does not affect DA1 or DA2.

## Task 5: Dynamic-Stdout Values

### Task 5a: Dispersion Figure (Fig. 1)

**Paper claims:** Ŝ(GMM) = 1.822, Ŝ(blob) = 1.022, H(GMM) = 5.14, H(blob) = 6.06.

**Investigation:** The figure script `figures/regenerate_all.py:compute_1d_ssa()` calls `compute_vector_ssa_score()` without passing `dt=`, causing `aggregate_ssa()` to fall back to S_old = mean(ρ²) instead of Ŝ = Δt × Σ ρ².

**Recomputed from saved raw ρ values** (`figures/dispersion_rho_dt01.csv`, 4000 lags, dt=0.01):

| Distribution | S_old (mean ρ²) | Ŝ (Riemann, no trunc) | Ŝ (1/N trunc) | Paper |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GMM | 0.0456 | **1.825** | 1.825 | 1.822 |
| Blob | 0.0256 | **1.023** | 1.023 | 1.022 |

**SSA values MATCH** the paper to within 0.003 (seed noise). The CSV was generated from a separate run with max_lag=4000 (not max_lag=500 as in the figure script).

**Entropy:** The `compute_1d_entropy()` function returns H in nats (2.112/2.836). Paper claims 5.14/6.06. No transformation of these values matches the paper. **Entropy values remain unresolved** — same conclusion as the main audit's entropy investigation. Likely from an uncommitted convention (e.g., bits, or a different estimator).

**Bug identified:** `compute_1d_ssa()` at line 204 does not pass `dt` to `compute_vector_ssa_score()`. The figure annotations would show S_old, not Ŝ. However, the paper text correctly quotes Ŝ values (verified from raw ρ data).

**Saved files:**
- `figures/data/dispersion_values.json` — S_old values (for reference; NOT the paper Ŝ values)
- Raw ρ data in `figures/dispersion_rho_dt01.csv` reproduces paper Ŝ values
